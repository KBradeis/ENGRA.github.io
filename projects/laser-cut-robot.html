Laser-Cut Robot

Soldered a robot's circuit board with my partner and designed its wooden chassis for the laser cutter.

Partner

Ian

Timeline

Spring 2026 · ENGR 11A

Tools

Soldering · CAD · LightBurn

Focus

Electronics & fabrication

Process

How it came together

Step 01

During the beginning of class I partnered with Ian. He and I got along instantly. He is a computer science and math major. I previously had soldered in Highschool for my electrical class. It wasn’t something that I still remembered how to do properly.

Step 02

I enjoyed relearning how to solder. It was a cool experience that showed me how something works. I might start trying to do little personal projects for fun in my own free time. I think the only thing that I need to get better at is making better joints and not getting the smoke in my face.

Step 03

Ian started most of the chassis structure in which all we had to do was rearrange the measurements so that it’s accurate to the model we have in class. I’ll probably end up doing most of the editing for our next version based on whatever’s wrong with this model we currently have.

Step 04

This is what it looks like when we are trying to print its. We place it in this orientation to make sure that everything is printed out correctly. Hoping that we don’t have to worry about the print getting messed up. We also changed the infill to be more so that it is more durable.

Step 05

This is my wood design for lightburn. Ian helped me figure out how to make it. The only thing that this might be lacking is the right measurements in terms of the width. I liked trying out all the features. I’m also very curious how difficult it is to make the joints manually instead of using the automatic feature.

Step 06

This is what our final Laser cut print looked like once everything was done. Ian decided that he could add some personal designs like a number plate. I think we did a good job with what we have going on.
